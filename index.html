With over 15 years of hands-on experience in photography, video content creation, and social media strategy, I know exactly what catches the eye. I don't just shoot pretty pictures and videos. I capture your stay through a strategic marketing lens to create imagery that actually drives bookings.

Joe and I travel full-time across Europe in our campervan, making it effortless to capture your stay on-site. I bring all my professional gear directly to your doorstep, no complex travel or hotel logistics required
